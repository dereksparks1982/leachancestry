Unknown / unresolved father research boundary
Why the line stops at Andrew: one Andrew profile points to William Leach Jr. (about 1813); another points to William Terrill Leach (1810–1891). The William Terrill profile itself mixes incompatible Massachusetts and Indiana records. Until the parentage is independently proved, everything beyond Andrew is kept in the hypothesis column.

What we're testing next

1. Andrew's death certificate

The Missouri State Archives has digitized death certificates. Parent names and informant data may identify Andrew's father and mother directly.

2. 1870 household

Andrew appears in Scott Township, Steuben County, Indiana. The entire household must be reconstructed, not just accepted from an Ancestry hint.

3. Land & Depression story

Trace deeds, mortgages, tax delinquencies and probate in Barry/Newton Counties to test the family story that substantial property was lost during the Great Depression.

4. DNA corroboration

Raw genotype data alone cannot identify the correct Leach branch. Match lists and cousin trees will be used to test competing parentage hypotheses.
