# Case File: Andrew Milton Leach (1856–1928)

Andrew is currently the most important person in the project because his identity is reasonably coherent while his **parentage is not**.

## Stable life pattern

The imported records consistently point to one man:

- **Name:** Andrew Milton Leach
- **Born:** 11 March 1856
- **Birthplace:** Angola / Steuben County, Indiana
- **Marriage:** 18 August 1874, Indiana, to Clarissa Emma Mowan in the imported tree
- **1870:** Scott Township, Steuben County, Indiana
- **1880:** Sampsel, Livingston County, Missouri
- **1900:** Shell Knob, Barry County, Missouri
- **1910:** Shell Knob, Barry County, Missouri
- **1920:** Shell Knob, Barry County, Missouri
- **Died:** 17 July 1928, Newton County / Neosho area, Missouri
- **Burial:** Shell Knob, Barry County, Missouri

The Ancestry GEDCOM includes references to federal census records, Missouri state material, a death certificate collection, a Neosho newspaper death item, FamilySearch and Find a Grave.

## The two competing parent links

### Candidate 1: William Leach Jr.

One Andrew profile gives:

- **William Leach Jr.**
- born about 1813, Massachusetts
- died before 1867, Whitley County, Indiana

This version is thinly sourced, but geographically it deserves investigation because it places the candidate father in Indiana.

### Candidate 2: William Terrill Leach + Elizabeth Arnold

The second Andrew profile gives:

- **William Terrill Leach**, born 20 Aug 1810 in Bridgewater, Massachusetts
- **Elizabeth Arnold**, born 21 Mar 1831 in Ohio
- marriage in the imported family record: 24 Apr 1850, Wabash, Indiana
- children listed as Sylvester, Sylvanus A., Andrew Milton and Amza M. Ann

The problem is that the imported William Terrill record is internally contradictory.

## Why William Terrill is currently rejected as a proved father

Independent genealogical work identifies William Terrill Leach of Bridgewater as:

- son of **Peleg Leach and Sally Gilmore**;
- husband of **Sarah Swift Washburn**;
- married 2 August 1835 in Wareham, Massachusetts;
- resident of Wareham in the 1850 and 1860 federal censuses;
- a machinist;
- died 27 December 1891 in Providence, Rhode Island.

Source: https://maltbyfamily.net/genealogies/washburn/washburn_plymouth_118.html

That documented Massachusetts trajectory conflicts sharply with the imported claim that the same man married Elizabeth Arnold in Indiana and fathered Andrew there.

The GEDCOM itself gives William Terrill two incompatible death entries:

- 27 Dec 1891, Providence, Rhode Island
- before 1867, Whitley County, Indiana

This is almost certainly a profile collision or bad merge.

## Bad attachments on Andrew's duplicate profile

The larger Andrew profile contains records that should be removed from consideration unless the original images prove otherwise:

- **1860 Monroe, Virginia census** — geographically inconsistent with the otherwise coherent Indiana childhood.
- **American Civil War Research Database** — Andrew was born in 1856 and would have been a child during the war.

These bad hints demonstrate why source count by itself is not evidence quality.

## Best next record: 1928 death certificate

The Missouri State Archives states that death certificates can provide date of birth, parents' names, spouse, occupation, funeral home and burial information.

Database: https://s1.sos.mo.gov/records/Archives/ArchivesMvc/DeathCertificates

The imported GEDCOM points to the Missouri Death Certificates collection for Andrew, so the actual image should be retrieved and transcribed. The parent names on that certificate will be important, but they must still be evaluated according to **who supplied the information**.

## Census reconstruction needed

### 1870

Imported citation:

- Scott, Steuben County, Indiana
- page 193B
- post office Angola

The research task is to transcribe the entire household and neighboring households, then identify whether Andrew is living with a mother, stepfather, siblings or relatives.

### 1860

The imported Virginia census attachment should be treated as a likely false hint. Instead, search Steuben, Whitley, Kosciusko, Wabash and adjacent Indiana counties for:

- Andrew Leach, age about 4
- William Leach
- Elizabeth Leach / Arnold
- Sylvester Leach
- Sylvanus Leach

## Decision rule

Andrew's father will be promoted only when at least two strong pieces of evidence converge, ideally:

1. a primary or near-primary record naming the parent; and
2. a household/probate/marriage/deed record placing the same family together.

DNA cousin matches can then be used as corroboration rather than as a substitute for documentary genealogy.
