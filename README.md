# Leach Ancestry Research

Evidence-first reconstruction of Derek's maternal **Leach** line, with special attention to the family's movement from Indiana into Missouri and Kansas, property ownership, and the unresolved question of whether this branch connects to the old Bridgewater, Massachusetts Leach family.

## Current conclusion

The uploaded Ancestry GEDCOM is useful, but it contains duplicate people and conflicting merges. The public research baseline is therefore intentionally conservative.

### Strong working line

The records currently support this deceased line with good consistency:

1. **Billie Bert Leach** (1926–2001), born Neosho, Newton County, Missouri
2. **Elmer Edger Leach** (born 1905), born St. Joseph, Missouri
3. **Arealis Bert Leach / Leech** (1880–1954), Missouri
4. **Andrew Milton Leach** (1856–1928), born near Angola, Steuben County, Indiana; later Missouri

The tree becomes **unresolved at Andrew's father**.

## The broken bridge

The GEDCOM contains two records for the same Andrew Milton Leach. One links him to **William Leach Jr. (about 1813)**. Another links him to **William Terrill Leach (1810–1891)**.

The William Terrill profile in the imported tree is internally contradictory. It combines Massachusetts and Indiana material and even contains two incompatible death statements. Independent published research identifies William Terrill Leach of Bridgewater as the son of Peleg and Sally (Gilmore) Leach, husband of Sarah Swift Washburn, living in Wareham, Massachusetts in 1850 and 1860, and dying in Providence in 1891. That makes the imported Indiana parentage connection highly suspect.

**Result:** the connection from Andrew Milton Leach to Peleg/Benjamin/Giles of Bridgewater is a hypothesis, not a proven lineage.

## Giles and Lawrence Leach

Even if the Bridgewater connection is eventually proved, the older claim that **Giles Leach of Bridgewater was a son of Lawrence Leach of Salem** is disputed by later genealogical research. Older family histories make that connection; modern researchers note that no credible primary evidence has established it.

Accordingly this repository keeps **Giles → Lawrence** in the disputed section rather than presenting it as fact.

## Privacy policy for this repository

This repository is public. To avoid turning genealogy into a privacy leak:

- raw AncestryDNA data is **not** uploaded;
- the complete GEDCOM is **not** uploaded;
- living relatives' birth dates, addresses, and other identifying details are omitted;
- only deceased ancestors and research conclusions needed to establish the historical line are published.

The original GEDCOM and DNA export remain private research inputs.
